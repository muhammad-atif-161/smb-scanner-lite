Reference / Method and measurement

Assessment method: authorization to evidence

A reproducible workflow for scope, domain verification, observation, classification, report retention and interpretation of public DNS/email security findings.

Online scan status

Define a legitimate target

Record the exact domain, the requested assessment profile and explicit authority to test. A temporary DNS TXT challenge helps verify control of the submitted name before a worker can claim its job. It does not replace contractual authorization or verify the identity of the requester.

Collect with bounded methods

Read only the public records defined by the scan profile. The online worker uses DNS/email checks, and must not silently follow into TCP port scanning or browser vulnerability testing. Report where selector-based discovery or DNS resolver behavior limits confidence.

Classify observed outcomes

Use pass, warning, fail, inconclusive or not tested according to the method's rule. A failed network request is not evidence that a control is absent. Do not manufacture confidence when data is incomplete.

Keep a private evidence record

Store the scan's target, profile, timestamp and findings. Report previews should expose only agreed high-level details. Full evidence remains protected until the authorized owner has the required access; payment unlocks are not currently in operation.

Re-evaluate after a change

Repeat the same observations from a comparable vantage. Note DNS propagation, different resolvers and policy changes rather than comparing two scores without context.

Service scope: The prepared online scanner observes public DNS/email policy only. This reference may discuss controls that are not in that live profile.
