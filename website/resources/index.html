Editorial standard

What this section will not do.

A knowledge base is only useful if it says something. These are the rules the articles follow.

  • No keyword fillerEvery article answers a question someone would actually ask, and links to the RFC or vendor documentation rather than paraphrasing it loosely.
  • No invented authorityNo fabricated citations, no made-up statistics, no claims of testing that did not happen.
  • No disguised product pitchArticles explain the topic first. Where the product is relevant, the link is explicit.
  • Corrections welcomeIf something here is wrong or out of date, the contact form reaches the maintainer directly.

Where to start

If you are new to this: begin with the external posture assessment definition, then read the SMB exposure survey.

If you are fixing email: go straight to SPF, DKIM and DMARC — it is the highest-value gap in most small businesses.

If you are evaluating the product: read the posture score article, then compare it with the methodology.
