Reference / Method and measurement

Reading a score without losing the details

A posture score is a summary within tested scope, not a security certificate. Learn how coverage, uncertainty and evidence should shape its interpretation.

Online scan status

The denominator is the story

Two identical scores may represent different sets of measured controls. Before comparing results, examine the profile, conclusive-control count, weights, timing and unresolved checks. A narrow DNS/email score must not be compared directly to a full perimeter assessment.

Uncertainty does not disappear into a grade

When a check could not run or a DNS response timed out, the result should be recorded as inconclusive or not tested, not silently treated as a pass. The report should make coverage visible enough for the reader to see what was—and was not—examined.

Use the number to prioritize work

Read each finding's observation, the proposed change and whether the conclusion follows from that evidence. Assign an owner, record the exact configuration change and verify the result after propagation or deployment.

Reject the false assurance trap

A strong score is not evidence that accounts are protected, backups work, application logic is safe or staff are trained. It is useful precisely because it describes a smaller, repeatable set of questions.

Service scope: The prepared online scanner observes public DNS/email policy only. This reference may discuss controls that are not in that live profile.
