Product questions, licensing and partnerships.

The focus of this website is the online assessment service. Software ownership, licensing or partnership proposals can be discussed privately without implying a customer base or revenue history.

What can be discussed

Licensing, technical collaboration and possible project proposals. Any arrangement would require its own written scope and due diligence. The hosted backend, website, historical demo and downloadable sandbox are separate parts of the project.

What is not being represented

No public offer claims established recurring revenue, customer contracts, independent certification or a proven commercial operating history. If you need a customer assessment, use the verified online portal instead.
