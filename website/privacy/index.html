Privacy policy

Effective October 2026 · Policy version 2026.10-sandbox-v1 · Maintainer Muhammad Atif.

This website is a static explanation of SMB Scanner Lite. It does not run scans, does not require an account, and does not sell personal information.

If you run the sandbox program on your own computer, consent receipts are written locally (by default under your home directory) and are meant to stay there. Network lookups go only to one of the five approved test domains you chose.

You agree not to bypass the domain lock, not to present a sandbox report as a formal commercial audit, and not to use the software against systems you are not allowed to test.

The software is provided as-is for learning and authorised defence. The author is not liable for misuse.
