Questions answered

Before you scan, know the limits.

Common questions

What is SMB Scanner Lite?

An evidence-first external posture assessment. It records selected public DNS and email observations for a domain after ownership verification.

Is this a vulnerability scanner?

No. It assesses selected configuration and exposure signals. It does not exploit, fuzz or attempt logins.

Can I scan any domain?

No. The online service requires a matching DNS TXT ownership challenge and authorization for that domain.

Does a good score mean we are secure?

No. A score summarizes only the controls in the selected profile. It is not a certification or security guarantee.

What is outside scope?

The Basic profile does not test application code, accounts, devices, internal networks, TLS, open ports or exploitability. See Coverage for the published boundary.

How do I prove control of a domain?

The service returns a time-limited TXT value to publish under _smbscanner-verify.yourdomain. It verifies an exact match before making the job eligible to run.

What if my DNS change takes time?

DNS may take time to propagate. The challenge expires after one hour and verification attempts are limited. Avoid repeated attempts while the record has not propagated.

How do results stay private?

Assessment details are served through the private assessment ID and access token. Reports are not published on the website.

What is the difference between online service and desktop sandbox?

The online service requires domain ownership verification. The Desktop sandbox (learning only) is a local Python tool limited to five named test domains.

Do you sell or publish scan data?

No. The service does not sell or publicly publish assessment data. See the Privacy Policy for what the online service stores.

Can I rerun a scan automatically?

No. A submitted job is designed to dispatch once. If its status is uncertain, the service does not silently submit another run.

SMB Scanner Lite
