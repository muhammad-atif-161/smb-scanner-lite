Validation & trust

Credibility from evidence, not from badges.

There are no customer logos, review scores or usage statistics on this page, because none are held with documented permission. What is published instead can be checked: a test record you can reproduce, a documented method, real sample evidence and an explicit list of limits.

Jump to the test recordRun the tests yourself
What this page does not do

It does not invent customer proof. It does not publish a test count that cannot be reproduced. A larger internal validation register is maintained in the private repository; its contents and results are not published here, and no figure from it is claimed on this site.

2 &middot; Technical methodology

The method is documented, not implied.

Measurement, statuses, scoring weights, grade bands, evidence handling and the boundary of the method are published in full on the methodology page.

What is observed

Public DNS records, mail authentication policy, TLS negotiation, HTTP response headers and protocol-level reachability of defined high-risk ports. Nothing authenticated.

Methodology

How it is scored

Weighted over conclusive controls: pass full credit, warning partial, fail none, inconclusive excluded. Category weights and grade bands are published.

Scoring

The control catalog

All 22 controls documented with what each evaluates, why it matters, an example of its evidence, and whether the public sandbox actually runs it.

4 &middot; Privacy and safety

What the site and the tool each do with data.

This website is static. The sandbox runs on your machine. Neither sells data, and consent is recorded locally before any run.

The website

  • No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking cookies
  • No account, no login, no newsletter list
  • Enquiry form fields are used to reply to that enquiry only
  • The demo loads static stored data; it never contacts a target
Privacy policy

The sandbox

  • Five approved domains, enforced before any network request
  • Consent receipt written locally, chained by SHA-256
  • Read-only checks; no login, no payload, no exploitation
  • Reports stay on the machine that produced them
Responsible use

Authorisation is not optional. The full engine is for domains you own or have written permission to assess. The MIT licence on the published code governs the software; it does not grant permission to test third-party infrastructure.

5 &middot; Product limitations

What it cannot establish, written down.

A security product that only describes its strengths is not giving you a usable picture. The limitations page states the boundary of the evidence in plain language.

Structural limits

  • No visibility behind authentication or inside the network
  • No proof that a weakness is exploitable
  • No ability to detect compromise, past or present
  • No coverage of people, process or physical security
Full limitations

Honest status of the project

  • No published customer base, and none claimed
  • No revenue claims, no usage statistics
  • Public sandbox is a demonstration edition, not a service
  • Assessment capacity is arranged directly and reviewed manually
Project status and acquisition

Reproduce the record, then judge the product.

The test suite and the recorded assessment are both public. That is the point.
