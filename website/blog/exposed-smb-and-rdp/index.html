When the office door faces the street

SMB and remote desktop do not belong on the public internet.

SMB is how Windows computers share files inside an office. Remote desktop is how someone sits at a machine from far away. On a private network, with signing and a firewall, they are ordinary. On the public internet they are an invitation.

The check used here does not guess from a half-open port. For SMB it sends a single negotiate and reads the dialect and whether signing is required. It stops there. No password, no share list, no exploit. If the service answers, the finding says so, and the advice is the same every time: close it at the firewall and reach it only through a private path you control.
