No claim without evidence

Why a security report should quote what it saw.

A lot of scanners turn silence into certainty. The packet never came back, so they mark a control failed. That teaches the wrong lesson: absence of an answer is not the same as a bad answer.

SMB Scanner Lite keeps a separate status for that case — inconclusive. A failure is reserved for something the tool actually observed: a DMARC policy of none, a certificate that has expired, a zone transfer that returned records, an SMB dialect that answered on port 445.

The phrase on the project is simple enough to say out loud to a shop owner: no claim without evidence.
