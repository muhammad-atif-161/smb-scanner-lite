Why email locks matter for a small shop

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC in one page, without the ceremony.

If your domain can be impersonated, a customer may pay an invoice that never came from you. SPF lists who may send. DKIM signs the message. DMARC tells the world what to do when those checks fail — monitor, quarantine, or reject.

A small business often inherits a half-written SPF record ending in “soft fail,” or a DMARC policy that only watches. Those are not disasters by themselves. They are unfinished locks. The scanner’s job is to show the record it found and what that record actually does to a sender who is not you.
