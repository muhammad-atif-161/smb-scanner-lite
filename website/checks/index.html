What is inspected

Twenty-two controls, told as five groups.

The full private engine accounts for every control in a catalog. A report never hides a skipped check: if something was not tested, it says so. The public sandbox demonstrates the same standard on the five allowed domains.

GroupWhat a visitor can learn
DNS integrityDoes the name resolve, is DNSSEC’s chain locally checked, can any certificate authority issue for you, can a stranger copy the whole zone, is an old name pointing at a cloud you no longer own?
Email authenticationDoes SPF actually fail unauthorised senders, does DMARC enforce, are DKIM keys long enough, and do your mail servers look intentional — including a domain that has chosen to receive no mail?
TransportWhich TLS versions answer, is the certificate name and expiry sound, and does HTTP redirect to HTTPS?
Browser headersHSTS, Content-Security-Policy, clickjacking, sniffing, cookies, CORS, referrer, and permissions — the instructions your site gives a browser.
Network exposureProtocol-checked reachability of high-risk ports, including SMB on 445 and remote desktop, without authentication or exploitation.
