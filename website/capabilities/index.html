Service scope

A focused scan, with a clearly defined boundary.

The online service is designed to inspect public DNS and email-authentication configuration for a domain that has passed ownership verification.

Open scannerHow findings are classified

What is checked

Four groups of observable signals.

These are categories of checks, not promises of exhaustive coverage.

01 / ONLINE ASSESSMENT

DNS visibility

Public resolution and selected integrity checks

We retrieve observable DNS records for the verified hostname. DNS answers can differ by resolver and time; a missing or timed-out response is not automatically a vulnerability.

02 / ONLINE ASSESSMENT

SPF

Sending policy

Inspect the published SPF policy for syntactic issues, multiple records, lookup complexity and whether its stated enforcement is meaningful.

03 / ONLINE ASSESSMENT

DMARC

Domain alignment policy

Check whether a DMARC record exists, parses coherently and requests monitoring, quarantine or rejection. Published policy is not proof of how all receivers behave.

04 / ONLINE ASSESSMENT

DKIM

Discoverable signing keys

Look for keys at a limited list of common selectors. An undiscovered selector is inconclusive, not proof that DKIM is absent.

Not included in this online release: port scans, TLS negotiation tests, website-header scans, application vulnerabilities, exploitation, login testing or internal network discovery. Broader controls belong to a separately scoped product, not this automated job.

Reading a result

What each status means.

A finding reflects an observation at a specific time.

01

Pass

Observed behavior meets the control's stated criterion.

02

Warning

Policy is ambiguous, limited or warrants review without proving a vulnerability.

03

Fail

Measured evidence contradicts a defined security control.

04

Inconclusive

The result could not be established with adequate confidence.
