Evidence first · Small business

See how a domain looks from the outside — and only what can be proved.

SMB Scanner Lite is an external security posture check for small and medium businesses. It looks at public email locks, DNS, encryption, browser headers, and accidental exposure of office services. Every finding carries the evidence behind it.

What it does

Five quiet questions a business should be able to answer.

01Can strangers send mail as you?

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC decide whether a fake invoice can look like it came from your domain.

02Is the address book signed?

DNSSEC, CAA, and zone-transfer checks show whether your domain records can be quietly rewritten or copied.

03Is the website’s lock modern?

TLS versions, certificates, and security headers tell a browser how carefully to treat your visitors.

04Is the office door on the street?

A protocol check looks for SMB file sharing, remote desktop, and common databases reachable from the internet.

05Is the score honest?

Timeouts stay inconclusive. A missing record is only reported when the answer was actually observed.

06Who is it for?

Owners and IT helpers at small firms who need a clear outside view — not an exploit kit, and not a substitute for a hired audit.

No claim without evidence. If the tool cannot see it, it does not invent a failure.

How it was made

Built in Python, scored in public rules.

Muhammad Atif built the scanner as a modular Python project: separate checks for mail, DNS, TLS, headers, and network exposure, then one scoring model that refuses to grade what it did not measure. The full engine stays in a private repository. This public site and the sandbox edition publish only the safe face.

Ethical lock

The public tool cannot roam.

Anyone who downloads the public edition can scan only five free testing domains: example.com, example.org, example.net, scanme.nmap.org, and badssl.com. A scan will not start until the person accepts the privacy policy and signs a consent proof stored on their own computer.
