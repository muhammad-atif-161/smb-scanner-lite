The service

Start with what an outsider can measure.

A public-domain review will not uncover every security problem. It can reveal policy and configuration gaps that should be understood, owned and fixed.

01 / DNS

Start with the records

How it works

Six stages, one carefully scoped assessment.

From proof of DNS control to a limited Basic result, each stage has a clear boundary. Paid profiles remain in private beta.

01

Enter your domain

Provide a public domain you control and explicitly authorize one DNS/email scan.

02

Verify DNS TXT

Publish a temporary record at your DNS provider. Exact proof is required.

03

Run scan

The one-time worker reads published DNS and email policy. No intrusive probes.

04

Restricted preview

See the evidence-based score, grade and selected findings at no cost.

05

Private report

A paid profile will include a private evidence report; access rules are still in private beta.

06

Export JSON

Standard and In-Depth are planned to include JSON download once released.

Service availability: The portal is prepared, but public scan creation remains disabled while the private deployment is finalized. You can explore the recorded example without registering or submitting a domain.

The report

An answer you can examine, not just a number.

The assessment records what the public DNS and email policy indicated at a particular time. Observations, statuses and limitations are as important as the summary score.

The useful question is not “Is our domain secure?” It is “What did we observe, and what can we do next?”
