Terms of use

A service with clear boundaries.

These terms describe use of the website, recorded demo, downloadable sandbox and prepared online scanning service. Last reviewed 4 November 2026.

Permitted purpose

Use the services only for legitimate defensive education and assessments of systems you own or are expressly authorized to test. You must not use the site for targeting third parties, bulk reconnaissance, exploitation or unauthorized access.

Service availability

Online assessments are not publicly available while the backend's operational controls remain disabled. Availability, report access and future pricing are displayed by the appropriate product page. The site must not be interpreted as a promise of an operating paid service until launch is announced.

Limits of assessment

The first online profile checks public DNS and email policy, not internal security or exploitability. Reports are informational, time-bound and affected by resolver and network conditions. They do not replace a professional penetration test or compliance audit.

Customer responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of submitted domain information, your authority to assess it, and the confidentiality of your access token. Do not share customer results without the domain owner's permission.

Desktop software

The public sandbox is available under the license published in the public repository and is restricted by its intended educational scope. Review its license before installation.

Payments, report delivery and refunds

Basic is planned as a free, limited profile. Standard and In-Depth are paid profiles planned to include a private evidence report and JSON download. They remain in private beta; prices, payment handling, taxes, refunds, disputes and support procedures will be displayed before checkout is enabled. No card payment is collected by the website today.

No guarantee

We work to present accurately observed findings, but do not warrant that an assessment detects every security risk or that a passing grade proves a system secure. The service may be unavailable, interrupted or declined for operational or abuse-prevention reasons.

Contact

For questions about these terms, use the contact page.
