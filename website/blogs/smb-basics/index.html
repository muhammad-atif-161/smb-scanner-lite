Blog · SMB security basics

Cybersecurity basics for small businesses

You do not need a security department to close the gaps that most small businesses actually have. You need a short list, someone with the authority to change a record, and a way to check that it stayed changed.

1 post Updated 31 October 2026 Cluster guide

Small businesses are not usually breached by something clever. They are breached by something ordinary that nobody owned: a mailbox that anyone could forge, a remote desktop port that was opened for a contractor in 2021, a subdomain still pointed at a closed hosting account.

The baseline guides here are written in that spirit. They assume one person wears five hats, budgets are real, and the goal is a defensible minimum rather than a perfect one.

Every post in this cluster ends with the same question: what would you do on Monday? Where a check can be run by hand, the command is in the post. Where it cannot, the post says so rather than implying a tool will handle it.
