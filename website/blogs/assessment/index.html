Blog · Assessment and scoring

Assessment, scoring and reports

A score is only useful if you can explain where it came from. These guides cover the method behind the number, what the number cannot tell you, and what to do in the week after you receive one.

4 posts Updated 31 October 2026 Cluster guide

Assessment is a genre with a credibility problem: plenty of reports, very little that a reader can check. The method here is deliberately narrow. It measures a fixed catalog of publicly observable controls, records the observation behind every conclusive finding, and states its limits in the same document as its findings.

That makes the output less impressive and considerably more useful. You can re-run a query, reproduce a finding, and tell whether last quarter's fix actually held.

Every post in this cluster ends with the same question: what would you do on Monday? Where a check can be run by hand, the command is in the post. Where it cannot, the post says so rather than implying a tool will handle it.
