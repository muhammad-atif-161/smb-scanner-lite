How delivery works on a static site

This website has no backend. The form validates your message and then either delivers it to the maintainer's form endpoint or opens your mail client with the enquiry composed — whichever is configured. If your mail client does not open, write to contact@smbscannerlite.me directly. Nothing is stored on this website.
