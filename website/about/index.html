About

A small-business tool, made by a student who wanted the honest version.

Large companies pay teams to watch their domains every day. A bakery, a clinic, or a family workshop usually does not. SMB Scanner Lite exists so that outside view — mail forgery, weak encryption, an office file share left open — can be explained without theatre.

See the capabilities Read the documentation

Purpose

Show a domain’s public security posture with proof attached to every line. Help an owner or their IT person know what to fix first. Teach the difference between a real finding and a guess.

Who it is for

Small and medium businesses, the people who look after their websites, defensive learners, and teachers who want a scanner that refuses to wander onto someone else’s network.

Who it is not for

Anyone hoping to probe arbitrary companies, break in, or print a scary report with no evidence. The public edition will not do that. The full engine is for authorised assessments only.

How it was made

The scanner is Python 3. Checks follow published standards — SPF (RFC 7208), DMARC (RFC 7489), DNSSEC (RFC 4034), TLS guidance, and an unauthenticated SMB2 negotiate that only reads dialect and signing. It does not log in, list shares, or exploit anything.

Scores use an evidence model with four statuses: pass, warning, fail and inconclusive. The full source, catalog and tests live in a private repository. This public repository ships the website and a sandbox whose allowlist is five domains long.
