About

A small-business tool, built by someone who wanted the honest version.

Large organisations pay teams to watch their domains every day. A clinic, a workshop or a family business usually does not. SMB Scanner Lite exists so that the outside view — forged mail, weak transport, an office file share facing the internet — can be explained without theatre.

See the capabilitiesCheck the evidence

Purpose

Show a domain's public security posture with proof attached to every line. Help an owner, or the person who looks after their IT, know what to fix first. Teach the difference between a real finding and a guess.

The product is deliberately narrow. It assesses a defined catalog of externally observable controls, scores what it actually measured, and states the limits of the method in the same report that delivers the score.

Who it is for

  • Small and medium businesses that need an outside view of their public exposure
  • IT administrators and managed service providers who need a defensible baseline
  • Cybersecurity consultants who want evidence rather than assertion
  • Defensive learners and teachers who want a scanner that refuses to wander onto someone else's network

Who it is not for

Anyone hoping to probe arbitrary companies, break in, or print a frightening report with nothing behind it. The public edition will not do that, and the private engine is distributed only for authorised assessments.

How it was built

Python 3. Checks follow published standards: SPF (RFC 7208), DMARC (RFC 7489), DNSSEC (RFC 4034), TLS guidance, and an unauthenticated SMB2 negotiate that reads only dialect and signing.

Scores use an evidence model with four statuses — pass, warning, fail and inconclusive. The full source, catalog and tests live in a private repository; this public repository ships the website, the documentation and a sandbox whose allowlist is five domains long.

Project status

Stated precisely, because vagueness would be misleading.

What is public

This website, the documentation, the recorded assessment, the privacy policy and a sandbox runtime hard-locked to five test domains, with its test suite.

What stays private

The complete engine with all 22 controls, the scoring model, the internal test suite and the development history. A public scanner that can be pointed at any host becomes a weapon.

What is not claimed

No customer base, no recurring revenue, no usage statistics and no testimonials are asserted on this site. Assessment capacity is arranged directly and reviewed manually.
