About the product

Built for decisions, not security theater.

SMB Scanner Lite is an independent project by Muhammad Atif, focused on making observable domain security controls easier to understand.

Why this exists

A domain carries more information than a business owner usually reviews: where traffic goes, who may send email for it and what recipients are asked to do with suspicious mail.

The aim is to make that public evidence legible, not to sell uncertainty as fear. The service records observed controls with stated limits, so the reader can ask a better question and request a specific fix.
